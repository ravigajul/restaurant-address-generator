Restaurant Address Generator

Pick a locale (e.g. en_US, en_AU) and get a brand-new restaurant address plus a VAT / tax ID. Click any field to copy it.

Everything is generated in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere. Addresses are fictional (real city + plausible postcode, random street). Tax IDs follow each country's real format and checksum, so they pass format validators, but they are not registered to any business — use for testing and demos only.
